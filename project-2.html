Industrial Rooftop

25 MW Manufacturing Facility Installation

Project Overview

A state-of-the-art rooftop solar installation for one of India's largest manufacturing facilities, enabling complete energy independence and zero-carbon operations.

Key Features

  • Smart energy storage system
  • Real-time monitoring and analytics
  • Automated cleaning systems
  • Grid integration capabilities

Technical Specifications

  • Total Capacity: 25 MW
  • Annual Generation: 45 GWh
  • Carbon Offset: 35,000 tons/year
  • Rooftop Area: 200,000 sq.m

Project Details

  • Location: Rajasthan, India
  • Duration: 18 months
  • Completion: 2023
  • Category: Utility Scale
