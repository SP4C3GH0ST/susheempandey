My Journey

As a multidisciplinary professional with extensive expertise in the energy, electrical engineering, renewable energy, and business development sectors, I have a proven track record of leading large-scale projects, developing innovative solutions, and driving business growth across India and international markets.

I specialize in solar power development, smart metering solutions, energy management, and regulatory compliance, with a strong focus on sustainability and efficiency. Throughout my career, I have consistently demonstrated an ability to successfully execute high-impact projects, navigate complex regulatory frameworks, and build strategic partnerships to foster growth and innovation.

1. Engineering and Technical Expertise

With a solid foundation in electrical engineering, I have managed and executed a wide range of projects, from solar power installations to smart grid systems and advanced metering solutions. My hands-on experience spans multiple domains within the energy sector, including:

Solar Power Projects: Over the years, I have spearheaded the development of solar energy projects totaling 1,804.135 MW globally, with a focus on both utility-scale and rooftop solar systems. In India, I have been involved in the implementation of significant solar projects such as:

  • 100 MW in Telangana (including 220 KV lines for grid connectivity)
  • 1,200 MW in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh
  • 600 MW in Bikaner, Rajasthan
  • 50 MW in Andhra Pradesh (Tadipatri)
  • 135 kW rooftop solar installations in Delhi and Chandigarh

Additionally, I have played a critical role in solar projects in Bangladesh, with 300 MW installed across multiple locations in Dhaka. These projects reflect my capacity to manage the technical, regulatory, and financial aspects of renewable energy, from concept to execution.

Transmission and Distribution Infrastructure: In parallel with solar power, I have been responsible for implementing transmission lines across different voltage levels. These include:

  • 33 KV, 66 KV, 132 KV, and 220 KV lines as part of various solar parks and grid connections
  • Specific projects include 10 MW in Bihar with a 33 KV distribution line, 24 MW in Punjab with a 66 KV line, and 100 MW in Telangana with a 220 KV transmission line

Smart Metering Solutions: I have led smart metering and Automatic Metering Infrastructure (AMI) projects across North India. Key achievements include:

  • Managing the installation of over 298,800 meters in projects across multiple states
  • Securing and executing contracts for 45,000 electronic meters for Manipur Electricity Board and 250,000 meters for Punjab State Electricity Board
  • Improving meter reading rates from 14% to 45% in two months for BSES Rajdhani Power Ltd.
  • Executing smart grid projects, including AMI-based systems and prepaid metering for government consumers in BSES Delhi

2. Business Development and Strategic Growth

As a business development professional, I have demonstrated expertise in identifying and capitalizing on emerging opportunities, driving revenue growth, and expanding market share. My experience includes:

Strategic Market Expansion: I have successfully led expansion efforts into new regions and international markets. In India, I spearheaded solar power and metering projects across 7 states, including Delhi, Bihar, Karnataka, Punjab, Madhya Pradesh, Haryana, and Uttar Pradesh. Internationally, I expanded into Bangladesh, developing solar projects and securing long-term contracts.

Total Solar Capacity Installed: 1,804.135 MW globally, with significant contributions in India (504.135 MW) and Bangladesh (300 MW).

3. Legal and Regulatory Expertise

Having served as a Bar Council Advocate, I have extensive experience navigating the legal and regulatory frameworks surrounding renewable energy and infrastructure projects. I provide legal guidance and support across the following areas:

  • Regulatory Support & Compliance: Ensuring that projects meet national and state regulations, including environmental compliance, energy regulations, and electrical safety standards. I have worked with regulators like the Delhi Electricity Regulatory Commission (DERC) and State Electricity Regulatory Commissions to ensure projects are compliant and meet all necessary regulatory standards.
  • Contract Negotiations and Dispute Resolution: I have drafted and negotiated critical contracts such as PPAs, EPC contracts, land acquisition agreements, and operation contracts for various energy projects. I am also experienced in handling disputes related to construction, operations, and project financing.
  • Project Financing & Due Diligence: I have led the due diligence process for solar and energy projects, ensuring financial and legal soundness before investment. I have also structured financial deals, raising capital for projects and securing funding from private equity and financial institutions.

4. Clean Development Mechanism (CDM) and Energy Efficiency Projects

With a focus on sustainability and carbon reduction, I have been actively involved in the Clean Development Mechanism (CDM) and Energy Services Companies (ESCO) projects. Some of my notable achievements include:

  • CFL-based CDM Project in Karnataka: Successfully completed the first-of-its-kind CFL-based CDM project, obtaining Carbon Emission Reductions (CERs) from the UNFCCC.
  • Energy Audits and Energy Management: Conducted energy audits and energy management for government establishments, improving their energy efficiency and lowering carbon emissions.
  • ESCO Registration with ICRA: Registered HPL India as an ESCO with ICRA and played a key role in the Group Metering Project in Madhya Pradesh.
  • Distribution Transformer Metering: Managed distribution transformer metering for BESCOM and Madhya Pradesh Paschim Kshetra Vidyut Vitaran Nigam Ltd. to improve grid efficiency.

5. Leadership & Team Management

Throughout my career, I have consistently demonstrated strong leadership skills, managing cross-functional teams and multi-disciplinary projects. Key leadership accomplishments include:

  • Leading sales teams of up to 12 personnel in companies like Havell's India Ltd., ensuring that all team members met sales targets and contributed to the growth of the business.
  • Managing engineering teams, ensuring efficient execution of large-scale projects, including solar power installations, smart metering initiatives, and energy efficiency projects.
  • Providing mentorship and guidance to junior engineers and project managers, fostering a culture of collaboration, innovation, and performance.
