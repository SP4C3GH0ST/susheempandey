Floating Solar Plant

150 MW Water-Conservation Solution

Project Overview

An innovative floating solar installation that combines renewable energy generation with water conservation, demonstrating the future of sustainable infrastructure.

Key Features

  • Water evaporation reduction
  • Enhanced panel efficiency
  • Aquatic ecosystem protection
  • Dual-purpose land utilization

Technical Specifications

  • Total Capacity: 150 MW
  • Annual Generation: 275 GWh
  • Water Saved: 32 million liters/year
  • Water Surface Area: 750 acres

Project Details

  • Location: Rajasthan, India
  • Duration: 18 months
  • Completion: 2023
  • Category: Utility Scale
